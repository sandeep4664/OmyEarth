Our Process

Four steps from funding to verified carbon impact

Every tree we plant is monitored, measured, and reported — from sapling to canopy.

1
🗺️
Scientific Site Selection

KFRI-guided native species selection for Kerala's soil types and microclimates. Every location is scouted for survival probability before a single sapling is ordered.

2
📍
GPS Geo-Tagging at Planting

Every tree gets an exact GPS coordinate logged at the moment of planting by our Kudumbasree field team. Your tree exists on a map — forever traceable.

3
📐
Bi-Annual Girth Inspections

Every 6 months, trained field staff measure girth at breast height (GBH). This data feeds directly into the IPCC Tier-2 biomass equation to calculate actual carbon sequestered.

4
📋
Verified Impact Reports

Donors and corporate partners receive periodic impact reports with per-tree survival status, carbon metrics, and geotagged photo evidence. No greenwashing. Just numbers.

Accountability Chain

Not one tree planted without a verified data trail

1

GPS Coordinate Logged at Planting

Exact latitude/longitude captured via mobile app by Kudumbasree field staff on Day 1. Immutable record from the first moment.

2

Species & Site Data Recorded

Native species name, land type, soil condition, and planting date logged against each GPS coordinate in our database.

3

Bi-Annual Girth Measurement (GBH)

Every 6 months, a paid field inspector measures Girth at Breast Height. Photos, measurements, and survival status uploaded to the system.

4

IPCC Tier-2 Biomass Calculation

GBH data feeds the IPCC Tier-2 allometric equation for tropical mixed forests. Output: verified tonnes of CO₂ sequestered per tree per year.

5

Impact Certificate Issued

Donor or corporate partner receives a signed impact certificate with exact coordinates, species name, GBH readings, and calculated CO₂ sequestered.

Frequently Asked Questions

The ₹150 covers sapling, planting labour, and initial setup. But the real investment is the 5-year maintenance: watering contracts, organic manuring (10 applications over 5 years), bi-annual girth inspections, and carbon reporting. The lifecycle cost per tree is ~₹750–800. Your ₹150 is your share of a long-term commitment, not a one-time transaction.

OmyEarth operates a dual-entity model. The DPIIT-registered startup delivers B2B sustainability services (verified plantation, carbon reporting, ESG documentation) — these are service fees, not CSR receipts. For donations with 80G tax benefit, the OmyEarth Trust handles that structure. Our team will guide your CSR and legal teams through the correct engagement path.

Every tree is GPS geo-tagged at planting. Every 6 months, a trained field inspector physically visits, measures the Girth at Breast Height (GBH), photographs the tree, and updates our database. You receive a bi-annual report with exact coordinates, live GBH measurement, and a survival confirmation. If a tree doesn't survive, we report it honestly — and replace it under our care cycle protocols.

We plant only native species selected in collaboration with KFRI (Kerala Forest Research Institute) based on site-specific soil type, microclimate, and biodiversity needs. This means trees that belong in Kerala's ecosystem — with significantly higher survival rates than exotics. Common selections include Teak, Mahogany, Indian Rosewood, Neem, Mango, Jackfruit, and other endemic species.

We use the IPCC Tier-2 methodology, the international standard for tropical mixed forest carbon calculations. The input variable is Girth at Breast Height (GBH), measured bi-annually by certified field inspectors. GBH feeds the allometric biomass equation for the species, producing a scientifically defensible estimate of CO₂ sequestered per tree. Output: a per-tree, per-year CO₂ figure that can be verified and audited.

Ground execution is carried out by Kudumbasree women's self-help groups (SHGs) — Kerala's established community network with over 4 million members. This approach creates direct, dignified livelihood for women at the grassroots level, embeds the plantation in the local community (improving long-term survival), and eliminates the fly-in-fly-out model that dooms most drives.

We plant on voluntary private land (landowners who grant 5-year planting access agreements), degraded community lands, and government-approved locations in Kerala. All sites are assessed for accessibility (for bi-annual inspections), soil quality, water availability, and biodiversity value before a single tree is ordered.

OmyEarth operates a dual-entity model. The DPIIT-registered startup (private limited) handles B2B corporate sustainability services and earns service revenue. The OmyEarth Trust (separate entity) holds 80G status for individuals seeking tax-deductible donations. This structure ensures we can serve both corporate ESG partners and individual donors with the right legal framework for each.
