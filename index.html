How OmyEarth Works

Most plantation drives plant and walk away. We plant, maintain, and prove impact — with data.

Scientific Plantation

We select sites using climate data, choose native & fruit-bearing species, and plant with precision — not just numbers. Every tree is geo-tagged with exact GPS coordinates from Day 1.

Beyond Carbon

Every Tree Does More Than You Think

A maintained tree is a living system. It fights climate change on six fronts simultaneously — carbon is just the beginning.

🌿
~21 kg
CO₂ absorbed per tree per year (mature)

Carbon Absorption

Through photosynthesis, every tree pulls CO₂ out of the atmosphere and locks it into wood, roots, and soil. A grove of 1,000 mature trees absorbs the equivalent of ~210 car trips from Kochi to Bangalore every year.

Carbon Sequestration Climate Mitigation ESG Verified
🌡️
2–8°C
Local temperature reduction under a tree canopy

Heat Island Reduction

Urban and rural areas with tree cover are measurably cooler. Tree canopies block solar radiation and release moisture through transpiration — acting as natural air conditioners that reduce energy demand and protect communities from extreme heat events.

Urban Cooling Heat Wave Protection Energy Savings
💧
75%
Reduction in surface runoff with forest cover

Water Cycle Restoration

Tree roots absorb rainfall and recharge groundwater tables. Their canopies slow rainfall impact, preventing soil erosion. Forested areas maintain year-round stream flow and dramatically reduce flood risk during Kerala's heavy monsoon seasons.

Groundwater Recharge Flood Prevention Monsoon Resilience

500+

Biodiversity & Ecosystems

Native trees are not just trees — they are entire ecosystems. Birds, insects, fungi, and microorganisms all depend on them. OmyEarth prioritises native species that restore indigenous food webs and bring back wildlife that has disappeared from degraded land.

Native Species Wildlife Habitat Food Web Restoration
🪱
3×
More organic matter in soil under forest cover

Soil Health & Fertility

Falling leaves create a natural mulch layer that feeds soil microbes, improves structure, and prevents erosion. Healthy soil stores carbon, grows food, and is the foundation of all agriculture. Degraded land becomes productive again within a few years of consistent planting.

Erosion Control Soil Carbon Storage Agroforestry
🌬️
60%
Particulate matter filtered by roadside tree rows

Air Quality & Human Health

Trees filter dust, PM2.5, and harmful pollutants from the air through their leaves and bark. Communities near green cover report lower rates of respiratory illness. For India's rapidly urbanising towns, tree buffers are among the most cost-effective public health investments available.

PM2.5 Filtration Noise Reduction Public Health
